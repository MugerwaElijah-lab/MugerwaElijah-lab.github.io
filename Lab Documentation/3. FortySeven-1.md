# Threat Intelligence Profile: Mysterious Elephant (APT-K-47)

## Executive Summary

**Mysterious Elephant** (also tracked by threat intelligence vendors as **APT-K-47**) is a South Asian cyber-espionage group active since at least 2022. The group primarily targets government and diplomatic personnel, leveraging spear-phishing campaigns (often themed around Hajj and official regional affairs) to compromise targets and exfiltrate sensitive files—with a distinct focus on WhatsApp communications, documents, and credentials.

  

The group's operational tradecraft features a mix of custom-developed tools, modified open-source utilities, and code recycled from or shared with neighboring South Asian APT threat actors, including Confucius, SideWinder, BITTER, and Origami Elephant.

  

## Threat Actor Overview & Operational History

- **Primary Aliases:** Mysterious Elephant, APT-K-47
    
      
    
- **Origin / Geographic Region:** South Asia
    
      
    
- **Activity Period:** 2022 – Present (Discovered by Kaspersky GREAT in 2023)
    
      
    
- **Targeting:** Diplomatic entities, government organizations, and military institutions (notably within South Asia / Pakistan)
    
      
    
- **Primary Motivation:** Cyber Espionage & Strategic Data Theft
    
      
    
- **Tooling Overlap:** Incorporates and maintains legacy components previously abandoned by Sidewinder, Confucius, BITTER, and Origami Elephant.
    
      
    

## Technical Analysis & Signature Tooling

### 1. Delivery & Initial Access

- **Exploitation of Known Vulnerabilities:** Early delivery chains relied on remote template injection and **CVE-2017-11882**. Modern campaigns utilize malicious WinRAR archives exploiting **CVE-2023-38831** to deliver custom payloads disguised as official PDF session minutes or diplomatic notices.
    
      
    
- **Vtyrei Downloader:** Recycled downloader module originally tied to Origami Elephant, utilized in early Mysterious Elephant intrusion sets.
    
      
    

### 2. Backdoor Execution & Persistence

- **ORPCBackdoor:** Custom backdoor communicating via Office Remote Procedure Call (ORPC).
    
      
    - **DLL Hijacking:** Utilizes the `version.dll` template via exported function `GetFileVersionInfoByHandleEx(void)` to execute under trusted process contexts (e.g., `MicrosoftServices`).
        
          
        
    - **Persistence Check:** Evaluates whether `ts.dat` exists in its execution path. If missing, it invokes COM interfaces for TaskScheduler to create a scheduled task named `Microsoft Update` and drops `ts.dat` to prevent redundant task creation.
        
          
        
- **Asyncshell (Asyncshell-v2):** A core custom backdoor maintained since 2023. Recent iterations (v2) upgraded command-and-control (C2) communication channels from raw TCP to encrypted HTTPS to blend with normal network traffic.
    
      
    
- **MemLoader HidenDesk:** Reflective PE loader deployed via intermediate stagers to execute secondary payloads in memory.
    
      
    - **Anti-Analysis Sandbox Evasion:** Checks system processes upon launch and immediately terminates if fewer than **40 active processes** are running.
        
          
        
    - **Covert Workspace:** Spawns and switches execution to a hidden desktop session named `MalwareTech_Hidden`.
        
          
        
    - **Persistence:** Drops a shortcut file into the Windows Startup autostart directory.
        
          
        
    - **Payload:** Decrypts embedded shellcode using an RC4-like algorithm to launch commercial remote access trojans (e.g., Remcos RAT).
        
          
        

### 3. Exfiltration Modules

The threat actor deploys specialized post-exploitation tools designed for recursive directory traversal and targeted data exfiltration directly over primary C2 channels:

  

- **Stom Exfiltrator:** Recursively scans non-system drives, `Desktop`, and `Downloads` folders. Latest variants specifically search for hardcoded WhatsApp folder paths to harvest shared attachments, images, and archives.
    
      
    
- **Uplo Exfiltrator:** Employs depth-first search algorithms to collect document extensions (`.TXT`, `.DOCX`, `.PDF`, `.XLSX`), certificates (`.PFX`), and contact cards (`.VCF`) using XOR-deobfuscated C2 endpoints.
    
      
    
- **ChromeStealer Exfiltrator:** Custom binary targeting browser-stored credentials and web session artifacts.
