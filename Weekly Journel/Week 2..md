# Key Takeaways
Analysis  of any form requires attention to detail. You can have all the fancy tools but if you don't know where to look and what exactly you are looking for you won't make any progress. 

Threat Intelliegnce is a collective effort from research, to searching and constantly updating resources like the NIST NVD of Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVE's) and CISA's Known Exposures and Vulnerabilities data base (KEV).

For Threat intelligence to be useful context and understanding of the environment to be protected is very important. It is also important to understand how to read and navigate the MITRE ATT&CK in order to have understanding of Tactics and Techniques and how can potentially be used to exploit vulnerabilities
