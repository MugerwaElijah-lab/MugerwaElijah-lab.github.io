## Key Takeaways
Analysis of any form requires rigorous attention to detail. You can have all the advanced tools available, but if you don't know where to look and what specific indicators you are hunting for, you won't make progress.

Threat Intelligence is a continuous, collective discipline: from targeted OSINT research to cross-referencing industry repositories such as NIST NVD (CVEs) and CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog (KEV).

For Threat Intelligence to be truly actionable, context and deep understanding of the protected enterprise environment are essential. Navigating the MITRE ATT&CK framework is critical for mapping adversarial Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTPs) to potential vulnerabilities and defensive controls.
